03

The project

Heads up

In d.design, the generative agent leads on handling multiple design tasks. In 2025 the Alibaba Design team introduced "skills" into agents, letting users and creators steer the agent toward more varied outputs and styles. Like most AI skills, using one means going back into the conversation, tagging that specific skill, and changing the output from there.

Pain point 1 · for creators

Lack of affordance and control

Skills creation eases the agent's learning, but text-based prompts lose granular control for the creator. One use case: a creator might be unsure during the process of building a customized tool, and end up AI-led.

Pain point 2 · for users

Requests can't be completed effectively

40% of d.design's users are beginners who talk through their thinking across repeated rounds of conversation, at significant token cost — without knowing the change they wanted was one small edit away.

"I wanted to change my package color to white so I could send a different version to my suppliers."

— Business owner

"I stayed late just to manually draft an infographic table that fits my presentation's vibe."

— Marketing team
Solution

Built the plug-in feature as an experience connecting canvas editing and AI with more visual operations — collecting the most frequent design requests into adjustable tools, avoiding re-prompting.

Video · Full plug-in experience (open panel → pick tool → adjust → result)
Highlight 001

The Tasks section was my proposal

The PRD identified gaps in the user experience where the flow breaks down. One of the sharpest: users who lose their conversation also lose access to everything they'd generated in it. A dedicated Tasks view lets them find past outputs directly, without digging back through chat.

Image · Main panel UI (annotated, close-up on Tasks view)
Highlight 002

Assisted in building a UI library

Plug-in features vary, so control panel components differ between them. I analyzed which official plug-ins needed new UI and designed those components from scratch. Controls were designed to align with what works best with d.design's agent, giving a seamless experience for milestone 1 testing.
