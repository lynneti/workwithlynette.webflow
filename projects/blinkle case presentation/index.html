Slide 1 — The Problem
65%

of users never came back

Users leave after auto-apply because the job is done in their mind — but the real work hasn't started yet.

Slide 2 — First Attempt

We tried email re-engagement

What we built: email flow → sign back in → type recent companies → download resume.

What happened: retention was still low. User interview data — the 1–10 scale results showed limited re-engagement.

Slide 3 — The Insight

How might we bring users back — not just to apply, but to stay?

The Coinbase analogy: they don't just trade crypto, they built an ecosystem.

"What if Blinkle wasn't just an application tool, but a study platform?"

Slide 4 — The Concept

Interview Intelligence Hub

AI reads each resume + job description, identifies skill gaps, matches resources to weaknesses, and generates a prep board per interview.

📸 Concept flow: Resume → AI analysis → Gap board → Matched resources
